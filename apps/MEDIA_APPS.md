# Media Apps

## Vision

Media apps should provide fast native tools for viewing, trimming, annotating, and exporting visual media.

## Apps

- `nodalix-photo`: image viewer/editor.
- `nodalix-video`: video player and simple trim/convert tools.

## Current status

Both apps are documented scaffolds.

## Priorities

1. Build `nodalix-photo` as a fast viewer first.
2. Research GStreamer-based video playback for `nodalix-video`.
3. Keep editing features non-destructive by default.
