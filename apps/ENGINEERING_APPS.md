# Engineering Apps

## Vision

The engineering suite should make Nodalix useful for technical work: CAD, unit conversion, scientific tools, plotting, airfoil analysis, structural tools, and CFD research.

## Apps

- `nodalix-cad`: native CAD prototype.
- `nodalix-lab`: scientific and engineering toolbox.
- `nodalix-units`: unit conversion and physical quantities.
- `nodalix-plot`: engineering plotting.
- `nodalix-airfoil`: airfoil analysis and visualization.
- `nodalix-structures`: beams, trusses, and section properties.
- `nodalix-cfd`: long-term CFD interface and sandbox.

## Current status

`nodalix-cad` already exists. `nodalix-lab`, `nodalix-units`, and `nodalix-plot` have initial Rust skeletons. Airfoil, Structures, and CFD are documented scaffolds.

## Priorities

1. Stabilize CAD basics.
2. Build shared units/quantities engine.
3. Make Plot useful for CSV and function plotting.
4. Keep CFD as research until backend strategy is clear.
